The people behind it

A father & a son, each doing what he does best.

Heritage Albums is two people. One brings your family's memories back to life by hand. The other built everything around him, so he never has to think about anything but the work.

The Craftsman

Thomas Woolf

The hands your memories are in.

For decades, Tom has been the person New Jersey families trust with their most irreplaceable things. He restores every photograph and every reel of film by hand, on professional archival equipment — and he meets every family in person.

The relationships came first; the craft is what keeps them. When your family's history is in his hands, it never leaves careful, local ones.

The Founder

Neal Woolf

The studio built around the craft.

Neal built Heritage Albums so his father could do the one thing he does better than anyone — the work. He designed the studio, the technology, and the way families find their way to Tom.

His job is simple: make everything around the craft effortless, so the craft is all his father ever has to think about.

Two people, each in his lane — so nothing is left to chance.

That's not a slogan. It's why the work is this good, and why your family's story is safe with us.
